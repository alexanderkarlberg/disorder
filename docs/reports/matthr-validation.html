MATTHR for NC and CC

DISENT's three-parton matrix element now covers γ, Z and W exchange for charged-lepton and neutrino beams of both charges. That makes disorder's projection-to-Born mode available at O(αs) for every NC and CC process. For photon exchange the result is bit-identical to the original, and all ten processes agree with NNLOJET and POWHEG-BOX-RES within statistics.

The matrix element

A FORM calculation of |M|² for lepton + quark → lepton + quark + gluon, resolved into lepton helicity and quark chirality, gives an exact identity (sympy check). Each helicity combination is DISENT's photon denominator times one of two pairs of squared invariants. With k and k′ the incoming and outgoing lepton, p1 the incoming and p2 the outgoing quark:

same helicity (LL, RR): (k·p₁)² + (k′·p₂)²
opposite helicity (LR, RL): (k′·p₁)² + (k·p₂)²

M(i) = C2(i)·QQ + C3(i)·QQ3
QQ = DISENT's original photon structure (numerator: sum of the four squares)
QQ3 = same denominator, numerator (k·p₁)² + (k′·p₂)² − (k′·p₁)² − (k·p₂)²

C2 and C3 are the per-parton couplings of F2/x and F3 in units of eq², the same combination that gives Y₊C2 + Y₋C3 at LO. Antiquarks flip the sign of C3. There is no separate longitudinal coupling: for massless quarks FL = F2 − 2xF1 carries the same electroweak factor as F2, so it comes with C2. At O(αs) the −y²FL contribution sits inside QQ, which holds the full y-dependence of the parity-even part, just as for photon exchange in the original DISENT. QQ3 is the parity-odd, F3-like part. The couplings come from a new routine, parton_couplings, that mirrors the structure-function code, so the real-emission events use exactly the electroweak setup of the inclusive part.

In the gluon channel the parity-violating piece is odd under exchange of the quark and antiquark. DISENT's phase space is symmetric under that exchange, so the piece integrates to zero and the channel keeps its original form. For photon exchange C2 = eq² and C3 = 0 exactly, so the arithmetic is the original one.

The derivation is in derivations/matthr/ in the repository.

Other checks and observations

  • Unit tests. The new test_matthr runs for 13 flag sets. It checks photon bit-identity, the C2/C3 decomposition, and that QQ3/QQ → Y₋/Y₊ in the initial- and final-state collinear limits. test_matrix_element now also checks parton_couplings against the quark-parton model. The full ctest suite (59 tests) passes, also from a clean clone.
  • Photon bit-identity. The P2B validation references at O(αs²) are byte-identical apart from the header stamp; they also call MATTHR through VIRTHR, COLFOR and SUBFOR. Yesterday's 10⁸-event O(αs) photon run was repeated with the new binary, and its histograms, VEGAS grids and cross section are identical.
  • Lowest ρE bin. With seeds 1–4 the photon-dominated processes were 0.3% above both references in the lowest bin. Independent seeds 5–12 agree with the references, so this was a correlated fluctuation of about 2.5σ in the lowest τ, B and ρ bins. The P2B weights near the singular region have heavy tails, so the quoted errors of the first bins may be slightly small.
  • Guards. -p2b now accepts -includeZ, -CC and NC neutrino beams up to NLO. At NNLO the other O(αs²) pieces (VIRTHR, CONTHR, MATFOR, …) are still photon-only and are refused with "Cannot do Z/CC in p2b beyond NLO yet".
