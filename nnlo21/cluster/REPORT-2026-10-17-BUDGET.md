**Definitions.**
- CPU = mean user+sys per job from time.log (as `cpu_summary.py`).
- Q = N(1e-4), N(1e-5) and N(1e-4) - N(1e-5) of the total row (E, G6: tau_zQ 0.05-0.5), each part with its NNLO weight. d2 enters only below 1e-4, so its N(1e-4) is 0.
- c = sd * sqrt(CPU), and its share of the sum over parts. K = (sum c)^2: with the optimal allocation (N_p proportional to sd_p/sqrt(CPU_p)) an error eps costs K/eps^2 core-h.
- f20 = fraction of seeds beyond 20 x 1.48 MAD from the median; "var" = their share of sum (x - mean)^2.
