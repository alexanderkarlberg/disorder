## 1. Set-up and conventions
- Code: bin6 = `$P/bin6/{sliced21,nlo31,tau2_nlo}` (P = /ptmp/mpp/akarlber/disorder-nnlo21), built from 7ddc208. Runs: `$P/runs/ours6/{G6W,G6P,Ex6,DISENT-ph}`, created by `mk_g6.py`, lists in `$P/runs/ours6/groups6.txt`. The pilot directories (`*-pilot`) are not used.
- G6: x = 0.1, Q^2 = 5000, mode 1 (tau_zQ bins 0.05-0.1, 0.1-0.2, 0.2-0.3, 0.3-0.4, 0.4-0.5 and their sum 0.05-0.5), plain slicing, `EW31 = "2 0"` (W-) and `"0 0"` (photon), soft table `$P/tables/soft.tab`. Units dsigma/dx dQ^2 [pb/GeV^2], tables below in 1e-6 pb/GeV^2.
  Parts: b1, b2 (below the cut, sliced21), lo, vi, kp, r (nlo31 mode 1), rcorr and d2 (r with TECHDIFF; TECHMIN 1d-10 1d-8 and 1d-11 1d-9 with TECHREF 1d-10 1d-8). b0 (LO) was not run for G6.
- Combination (as combine5/combine_tcut): plain equal-weight mean over seeds, error = seed scatter / sqrt(N), parts in quadrature. NLO = b1 + lo (O(alpha_s^2) coefficient); NNLO = b2 + vi + kp + r + rcorr, plus d2 for tau_cut < 1e-4 (as in bin5); `NNLO(no d2)` is also in the tables.
- NLO references. W-: the MPP `runs/ccw1` numbers of CLUSTER-INSTRUCTIONS.md lines 33-36 (0.05-0.1 7.606e-4 +- 3.1e-6, 0.1-0.2 5.873e-4 +- 1.4e-6, 0.2-0.3 2.374e-4 +- 6.4e-7, 0.3-0.4 1.212e-4 +- 3.9e-7, 0.4-0.5 7.177e-5 +- 2.7e-7, sum 1.7783e-3 +- 2.8e-6) are used as given, i.e. already in the units of our output.
  Ambiguity: the instructions call them "NLO coefficient, x dsigma/dx dQ^2 units as combine_tcut"; combine_tcut prints DISENT's raw NLO coefficient divided by x. The given numbers agree with our NLO within 1% as they stand (a further division by x = 0.1 would be off by a factor 10), so I read them as the already divided combine_tcut numbers.
  Photon: `DISENT-ph`, 40 seeds x 3M events (`$P/bin6/tau2_nlo -x 0.1 -Q2 5000 -nev 3000000 -seed1 N`, disorder's own DISENT, no -CC). Per bin the plain mean over the 40 seeds of the NLO coefficient (field `ref` of tau2_nlo.dat) divided by x, error = seed scatter/sqrt(40) (not the per-event variance of tau2_combine.py). Values (raw coefficient / x): see the pull rows.
- Slopes: (N(a) - N(b))/ln(a/b), a > b, in % of the bin's NLO. The NLO in the denominator is the DISENT reference / x of the bin (not our NLO at the given tau_cut, which still contains power corrections at the larger tau_cut). In brackets the pull (N(a) - N(b))/sqrt|ea^2 - eb^2| (tau_cut points share events, as in the bin5 report). Negative slope: the NNLO rises as tau_cut decreases.
- Ex6: E (event shape tau_zQ in the inclusive cuts, `ZFIX=3`); plain parts (b0, b1, lo, b2, vi, kp, r, rcorr, d2) from the bin5 runs `$P/runs/ours5/E/` (the same files REPORT-2026-10-12 used: 16, 200, 200, 100, 250, 200, 3500, 400, 200 seeds); extras from `$P/runs/ours6/Ex6/{lo-x,vi-x,kp-x,r-x}` (`ZFIX=3 P2BSLICE=1 P2BEXTRA=1 VTARGET=0`; 200, 250, 200, 2000 seeds). NNLO_P2B = NNLO + vi-x + kp-x + r-x, NLO_P2B = NLO + lo-x (as combine5). `NNLO_P2B(bin5 extras)` uses `$P/runs/ours5/E/*-x` (50, 62, 50, 875 seeds) and reproduces the numbers of REPORT-2026-10-12 (checked). Units pb. NNLOJET reference `results/nnj5/E` (cross 59.493 +- 12 pb, per-bin tauzQ histograms times the bin width).
