**Own cancellation:** 68 r attempts were killed by our own cancellation of a duplicate r array (18 Oct 22:55) and were resubmitted.

**Double runs (found 19 Oct 16:10)**

- **Cause:**
  - 81 of the 1610 et tasks cancelled at 13:05 had in fact started late and kept running after scancel, on nodes stuck in COMPLETING.
  - The feeder had resubmitted the same seeds elsewhere, so two processes wrote the same `.out.tmp`.
- **Effect:** 24 r files held both runs (434 SCELL lines, 2 RESULT lines). The second process exited 97.
- **Fix:**
  - The 24 files were moved to `$P/Z05/quarantine/*.out.bad` and the seeds were rerun (array 49073962, all 24 clean).
  - The other 57 are single complete runs.
- **Guard:** `run_z05.sh` now leaves a seed alone if its `.out.tmp` was written within the last 10 min, and accepts a file only with exactly one RESULT line.
- **Interim report:** its summary (10:52) predates this incident; no double-run file existed then.
