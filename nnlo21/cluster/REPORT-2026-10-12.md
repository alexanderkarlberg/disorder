## 6. Conclusions and open points
- E: NNLO plain agrees with NNLOJET (59.5 +- 12) only at tau_cut <= 3e-5 (95.6 +- 21; 73.6 +- 34 at 1e-5); at 1e-4 it is 108 +- 15 (+2.5 sigma, worst bin +2.2), at 2e-3 176 +- 7.5 (+8 sigma); the per-bin window sum is +3.7 sigma.
  P2B agrees at all tau_cut (window sum -0.1 sigma) with errors of 35-65 pb. LO agrees (-0.8 sigma total; worst bin -2.3), NLO plain at 1e-5: 250.3 +- 0.9 vs 251.5 +- 0.7.
- Z: NLO agrees (total |pull| <= 1.8 for tau_cut <= 1e-4; worst bin -2.3 at 3e-5). NNLO total is 2.6-3.4 sigma above NNLOJET for tau_cut 1e-3 to 3e-5 (15-20 vs 10.9 +- 1.2 pb), 2.5 sigma at 1e-5 (20.4 +- 3.6,
  d2 +13.4 +- 1.4 included), the largest bin deviations in ptavg 30-60 (+5 sigma) and high Q^2/m12; it is 1.1 sigma at tau_cut >= 2e-3 (small statistics, coarse tau_cut). d2 is necessary at 1e-5 and does not remove the excess.
- W: NNLO total 0.272 +- 0.018 vs 0.176 +- 0.056 pb at 1e-4 (+1.6 sigma), 0.36 +- 0.05 at 1e-5 (+2.5 sigma, with d2); per-bin pulls up to +5.8 (ptavg 30-60). The NNLOJET W NNLO error is 30%.
  LO total differs by 0.03% (6 sigma): small, but beyond the statistical errors, unexplained (table interpolation? flavour sums?).
- NNLOJET bin errors rest on 2-12 seeds for several parts (V, VV_*, RV_6/7, and R, LO for E), so pulls are indicative; the Z/W excess is suggestive, not established.
- Open: (i) bin5 lo non-bit-identity with bin4 (unexplained); (ii) Z/W (and B) NNLO drifts up toward small tau_cut, E plain drifts down; (iii) E P2B extras too noisy to separate (more r-x seeds would help);
  (iv) E at 1e-5: error 34 pb, rcorr 105.9 +- 19 (mean/median 80.9) dominates; (v) W LO 6 sigma; (vi) NNLOJET per-part seed statistics.
